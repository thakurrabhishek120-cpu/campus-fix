# 🏫 CampusFix – Smart Campus Maintenance System

CampusFix is a smart campus maintenance platform that helps students report college issues, track complaint status, and receive resolution updates. Maintenance staff can manage complaints, prioritize urgent issues, and update their resolution status.

## ✨ Features

- 📝 **Report Issues:** Students can report campus problems easily.
- 📊 **Track Complaints:** Track complaint status from submission to resolution.
- 👨‍🎓 **Student Dashboard:** View and manage submitted complaints.
- 🛠️ **Staff Dashboard:** Manage complaints and update their status.
- 🔐 **Authentication:** Separate login access for students and staff.
- ⚡ **Priority Management:** Identify and prioritize urgent issues.
- ✅ **Resolution Updates:** Keep students informed about complaint progress.

## 🎯 Problem Statement

Students often face difficulties reporting campus maintenance issues and tracking whether those issues have been resolved. Traditional complaint systems can be slow and lack transparency.

## 💡 Our Solution

CampusFix provides a centralized digital platform where students can report problems and monitor progress, while maintenance staff can efficiently manage and resolve complaints.

## 🛠️ Built With

## 🚀 How It Works

1. Students log in to the platform.
2. Students submit a complaint with issue details and location.
3. Maintenance staff review and manage the complaint.
4. Staff update the complaint status.
5. Students track progress until the issue is resolved.

## 🌟 Benefits

- Better communication between students and staff
- Improved transparency
- Efficient maintenance management
- A better campus experience

CampusFix aims to make college campuses smarter, cleaner, safer, and more efficient by simplifying the maintenance complaint process.
